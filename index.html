About Me

With over 14 years of experience engineering enterprise .NET applications, my focus is on ensuring architectural resilience and data integrity within highly regulated, complex domains.

I specialize in the modernization of legacy infrastructures, incrementally decoupling monolithic codebases into maintainable, scalable architectures. My core engineering pillars include:

  • Regulated Domain Expertise: Engineering high-compliance, data-intensive applications (EHR) where strict transactional consistency and data integrity are non-negotiable.
